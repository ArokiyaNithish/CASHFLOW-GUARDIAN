# Loan vs Financing Comparison

When facing a cash shortfall, MSMEs have different options for raising capital. Comparing Invoice Financing to Short-term Loans is critical.

**Invoice Financing (Discounting / Factoring)**
- **Cost:** Typically 1.5% to 2.5% monthly discount fee.
- **Speed:** Very fast. Funds are usually disbursed within 24-48 hours.
- **Collateral:** No separate collateral needed; the invoice itself acts as the asset.
- **Best For:** Immediate, short-term cash flow gaps caused by delayed payments from reputable buyers.

**Short-Term Loan (Working Capital Loan)**
- **Cost:** Typically 12% to 18% annual interest rate.
- **Speed:** Slower. Processing and disbursement can take 5-7 days or more depending on bank procedures.
- **Collateral:** Usually requires collateral (property, equipment, or fixed deposits) and personal guarantees.
- **Best For:** Funding inventory purchases for a large upcoming order, seasonal cash needs, or general working capital expansion over several months.

**Decision Matrix:** Use invoice financing for speed and specific receivables. Use short-term loans for lower annualized cost over a slightly longer period when collateral is available.
