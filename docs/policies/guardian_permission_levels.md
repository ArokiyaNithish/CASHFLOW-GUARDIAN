# Guardian Permission Levels

CashFlow Guardian operates under strict permission levels to ensure security and managerial oversight.

Levels:
- **L1 Green (Informational & Analytical):**
  - The system has automatic permission to analyze data, update forecasts, and generate internal alerts. No human intervention is needed for these read-only operations.
- **L2 Yellow (Drafting & External Comms):**
  - The system requires owner or finance manager approval before any external action is finalized.
  - This includes sending drafted payment reminder emails, submitting financing applications, or communicating with suppliers. The system prepares the action; the human clicks "Approve & Send."
- **L3 Red (Strictly Blocked):**
  - The system is entirely blocked from direct financial movements.
  - Guardian cannot initiate bank transfers, modify banking credentials, or accept binding loan offers automatically. These actions must always be performed manually by authorized personnel outside the Guardian system.
