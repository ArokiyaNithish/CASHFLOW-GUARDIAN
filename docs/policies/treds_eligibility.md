# TReDS Platform Eligibility

The Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS) is an electronic platform instituted by the RBI to facilitate the discounting of trade receivables of MSMEs from corporate buyers. It enables MSMEs to receive prompt payments against their invoices.

Eligibility Criteria:
1. **MSME Registration:** The seller must be a registered Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME) with a valid Udyam Registration Certificate.
2. **Buyer Onboarding:** The buyer (corporate, government department, or PSU) must be onboarded on the respective TReDS platform.
3. **Invoice Age:** The invoices submitted for discounting should generally not be older than 90 days from the date of issuance, although specific platforms might have slight variations.
4. **Minimum Amount:** The minimum invoice amount eligible for discounting is typically ₹50,000, ensuring viability for the financiers on the platform.
5. **Acceptance:** The buyer must accept the invoice on the platform before it becomes available for bidding by financiers.

Benefits:
- Competitive interest rates due to multiple financiers bidding.
- Without recourse financing for MSMEs (the risk of buyer default is transferred to the financier).
- Improves cash flow and reduces working capital stress for MSMEs.
