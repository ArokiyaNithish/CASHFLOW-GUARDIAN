# Receivables Collection Guide

A structured approach to collecting receivables improves cash flow and reduces bad debt. Follow this timeline for overdue invoices.

Collection Timeline:
- **Day 1-7 Overdue: Gentle Reminder.**
  - Send an automated, polite email reminder. Assume the invoice was overlooked.
  - *Example:* "Friendly reminder that invoice #X was due on [Date]."
- **Day 8-15 Overdue: Follow-up Call.**
  - A finance team member should make a personal phone call to the client's accounts payable department to check the status and resolve any potential issues (e.g., missing purchase orders).
- **Day 16-30 Overdue: Escalate to Manager.**
  - The account manager or business owner should contact their counterpart at the client company. A firmer email stating the impact of the delay should be sent.
- **Day 30+ Overdue: Legal Notice / Escalation.**
  - Consider sending a formal legal notice.
  - Review the MSME Payment Norms policy to calculate applicable delayed payment interest and inform the client. Stop further supply/services until dues are cleared.
