# Early Payment Request Policy

Managing cash flow effectively sometimes requires requesting early payments from customers. This policy outlines when and how to make these requests.

When to Send an Early Payment Request:
1. **Overdue Invoices:** If an invoice is more than 7 days overdue, it is appropriate to request immediate or early payment.
2. **Cash Gap Anticipation:** If cash flow projections indicate a significant cash gap within the next 20 days, proactive requests for early payment can mitigate the shortfall.

Communication Guidelines:
- **Professional Tone:** Always maintain a professional and courteous tone. Avoid aggressive language, even for overdue invoices.
- **Value the Relationship:** Mention the value of the ongoing business relationship. Expressing gratitude for past business can soften the request.
- **Incentives (Optional):** Consider offering a small discount (e.g., 1-2%) for early payment, especially if the cash gap is critical.
- **Clarity:** Clearly state the invoice number, date, amount, and the reason for the request (e.g., "to support our current working capital needs").
- **Follow-up:** If there is no response within 3-4 days, a polite follow-up call is recommended over another email.
