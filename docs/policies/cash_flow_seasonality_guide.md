# Cash Flow Seasonality Guide

Understanding seasonal trends is crucial for accurate cash flow forecasting and preparation. Different periods of the year put varying pressures on liquidity.

Key Seasonal Trends:
1. **Festive Season (Oct-Nov):**
   - Typically sees a surge in sales. Expect receivables to increase by up to 40%.
   - Preceding this, payables often rise by 30% as inventory is built up. Ensure adequate financing is in place in Aug-Sep.
2. **GST Quarter Ends (March, June, September, December):**
   - The end of financial quarters, especially March (financial year-end), often involves significant tax outflows. Cash reserves must be planned accordingly.
3. **Agriculture Cycles (Kharif/Rabi):**
   - For businesses linked to agriculture, cash inflows are highly dependent on the Kharif (autumn) and Rabi (spring) harvest seasons.
4. **Monsoon Impact (Jun-Sep):**
   - Certain sectors (construction, logistics) may see reduced activity, leading to slower cash inflows but sustained fixed costs.

Preparation Strategy: Build cash reserves during peak inflow months to sustain operations during known cyclical downturns. Avoid committing to major capital expenditures just before a known low-cash season.
