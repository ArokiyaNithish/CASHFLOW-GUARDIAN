# Safety Reserve Policy

A safety reserve (or cash buffer) is essential for business continuity during unforeseen circumstances or economic downturns. This policy defines the minimum recommended safety reserve levels based on the MSME classification.

Target Reserve Levels:
- **Micro Enterprises:** Maintain a reserve equivalent to 1 month of recurring obligations (payroll, rent, utilities, EMIs).
- **Small Enterprises:** Maintain a reserve equivalent to 6 weeks of recurring obligations.
- **Medium Enterprises:** Maintain a reserve equivalent to 2 months of recurring obligations.

Critical Rules:
1. **Minimum Threshold:** The business should never allow the cash balance to drop below 50% of the calculated safety reserve target, except in extreme emergencies.
2. **Replenishment:** If the reserve falls below the target level, replenishing it should become a top financial priority in the following months.
3. **Liquidity:** The safety reserve should be held in highly liquid accounts (e.g., current accounts or short-term fixed deposits without withdrawal penalties) to ensure immediate access when needed.
4. **Regular Review:** The target amount should be recalculated quarterly to reflect changes in recurring expenses.
