# Risk Mitigation Strategies

Proactive risk mitigation protects the company from severe cash flow disruptions caused by external factors.

Key Strategies:
1. **Customer Concentration Risk:**
   - Avoid over-reliance on a single client. No single customer should account for more than 25% of total accounts receivable.
   - Actively work to diversify the client base across 10 or more active customers.
2. **Credit Insurance:**
   - Consider utilizing trade credit insurance, such as policies from ECGC (Export Credit Guarantee Corporation) for exports or domestic equivalents. This protects against bad debts if a major buyer defaults or goes bankrupt.
3. **Thorough Credit Checks:**
   - Before offering credit terms to new customers, perform thorough background and credit checks. Start with shorter terms (e.g., 15 days) and lower limits, gradually increasing them based on payment history.
4. **Contractual Protections:**
   - Ensure all sales contracts explicitly state payment terms, late payment penalties, and dispute resolution mechanisms.
