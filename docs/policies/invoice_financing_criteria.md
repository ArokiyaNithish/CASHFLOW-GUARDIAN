# Invoice Financing Criteria

Invoice financing provides immediate cash flow against outstanding invoices, helping businesses manage working capital gaps.

Standard Eligibility and Terms:
- **Invoice Age:** Invoices must typically be outstanding for more than 30 days to qualify.
- **Buyer Credit Rating:** Financiers usually require the buyer to have a credit rating of B+ or higher, ensuring a reasonable probability of repayment.
- **Financing Value:** The maximum financing amount is generally capped at 90% of the total invoice value. The remaining percentage is held back and paid (minus fees) once the buyer settles the invoice.
- **Processing Time:** The process is typically swift, with funds often available within 24 to 48 hours of approval.

Important Considerations:
- Businesses should evaluate the cost of financing (interest and fees) against the benefits of immediate cash availability.
- It is a short-term solution and should not replace solid credit control and collection practices.
- Strong relationships with reputable buyers improve eligibility and the terms offered by financiers.
- Different financiers may have slightly varying criteria, so comparing options is advisable.
