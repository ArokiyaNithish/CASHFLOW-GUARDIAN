# Government Schemes for MSMEs

Various government schemes exist to support MSME growth and provide emergency liquidity. Awareness and utilization of these schemes are vital.

Key Schemes:
1. **CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises):**
   - Provides collateral-free credit facilities (term loans and working capital) up to ₹2 Crore from eligible banks. The trust guarantees a large portion of the loan, making banks more willing to lend.
2. **Mudra Yojana (PMMY):**
   - Provides loans up to ₹10 Lakhs to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises. Categories: Shishu (up to 50K), Kishore (50K - 5 Lakhs), and Tarun (5 Lakhs - 10 Lakhs).
3. **ECLGS (Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme):**
   - Introduced during crises (like COVID-19) to provide emergency credit lines, often up to 20% of the borrower's total outstanding credit or turnover, fully guaranteed by the government.
4. **Udyam Registration:**
   - Mandatory Prerequisite. Having an active Udyam registration is essential to access priority sector lending rates, delayed payment protection, and all government schemes mentioned above.
