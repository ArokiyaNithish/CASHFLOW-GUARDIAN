# Working Capital Management

Effective working capital management is vital for the operational health of the business. It involves optimizing the balance between current assets (receivables, inventory, cash) and current liabilities (payables, short-term debt).

Key Metrics and Practices:
1. **Receivables Aging:** Monitor accounts receivable in 30-60-90 day buckets. Aggressively pursue invoices crossing the 60-day mark to prevent them from becoming bad debts.
2. **Supplier Payment Terms:** Optimize payment terms with suppliers. Negotiate longer terms where possible without incurring penalties or damaging relationships. Match payables with expected receivables.
3. **Current Ratio:** Aim to maintain a Current Ratio (Current Assets / Current Liabilities) of at least 1.5x. This indicates a healthy ability to cover short-term obligations. A ratio below 1.0x signals potential liquidity issues.
4. **Inventory Optimization:** Minimize excess inventory, which ties up cash. Implement just-in-time practices where feasible.
5. **Cash Flow Forecasting:** Maintain a rolling 13-week cash flow forecast to anticipate needs and surpluses.
