# Crisis Prevention Playbook

When the system forecasts a significant cash deficit, follow this step-by-step playbook to mitigate the crisis before it occurs.

Playbook Steps:
- **Step 1: Early Identification (20+ Days Ahead):**
  - Act immediately when a gap is identified 20 or more days out. Do not wait for the gap to materialize.
- **Step 2: Contact Customers (Accelerate Inflows):**
  - Identify late-paying customers. Send polite but firm reminders. For large invoices due around the gap date, request early payment, offering a small discount if necessary.
- **Step 3: Negotiate with Suppliers (Delay Outflows):**
  - Identify flexible suppliers. Request a 15-30 day payment deferral for upcoming payables. Ensure this doesn't trigger severe penalties or stop critical supplies.
- **Step 4: Activate Financing (Bridge the Gap):**
  - If the projected gap remains over ₹2 Lakhs after steps 2 and 3, immediately initiate invoice financing for eligible invoices to secure cash quickly.
- **Step 5: Alert Board/Management:**
  - If the gap cannot be closed using operational levers or short-term financing, alert the board or senior management immediately to explore structural solutions (e.g., equity injection, long-term loans).
