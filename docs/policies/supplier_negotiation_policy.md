# Supplier Negotiation Policy

Negotiating payment terms with suppliers is a critical strategy for managing cash outflows, especially during tight cash flow periods.

Negotiation Tiers Based on Supplier Flexibility:
- **Highly Flexible Suppliers:** Target deferring payments by 15-30 days. These are often long-term partners with whom you have a strong track record.
- **Medium Flexibility Suppliers:** Aim for a 10-15 day deferral.
- **Low Flexibility Suppliers (or Critical Vendors):** Limit requests to 0-5 days or avoid deferring if it risks supply chain disruption.

Key Principles:
- **Penalty Awareness:** Before negotiating, review contracts for late payment penalties. Ensure the cost of the penalty does not outweigh the benefit of the cash flow extension.
- **Relationship Preservation:** Maintain transparent communication. Explain the temporary cash flow situation without sounding desperate. Assure them of your commitment to the partnership.
- **Proactive Approach:** Initiate negotiations well before the invoice due date. Last-minute requests can damage trust.
- **Offer Alternatives:** If a full deferral is not possible, propose a partial payment plan.
- **Documentation:** Always confirm any agreed-upon changes to payment terms in writing.
